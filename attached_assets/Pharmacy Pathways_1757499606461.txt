Feature
	BPharm – Bachelor of Pharmacy
	MPharm – Master of Pharmacy (Integrated)
	PharmD – Doctor of Pharmacy
	Typical Duration
	4 years
	UK/Ireland: 4 years (UK) or 5 years (Ireland); EU: 5 years in some countries
	EU (Hungary, etc.): 5 years; US/Canada: 6 years (2 pre‑pharmacy + 4 professional)
	Entry Point
	Direct from high school
	Direct from high school
	EU: Direct from high school; US/Canada: after pre‑pharmacy coursework
	Academic Level
	Undergraduate bachelor’s degree
	Undergraduate integrated master’s (UK/EU); sometimes postgraduate in Asia
	Professional doctorate (entry‑level)
	Primary Focus
	Pharmaceutical sciences, formulation, basic pharmacy practice
	Balanced: science + clinical pharmacy + placements
	Clinical pharmacy, patient care, therapeutics, interprofessional healthcare
	Clinical Exposure
	Limited; usually final‑year internship
	Significant placements throughout program (UK: ~1,000 hours)
	Extensive clinical rotations (US: ~1,740 hours; EU: substantial placements in final years)
	Research Component
	Minimal to moderate
	Final‑year research project/dissertation
	Optional; focus on clinical practice (research possible in electives)
	Licensing Outcome
	Eligible for pharmacist registration in countries recognising BPharm
	Meets EU Directive requirements for pharmacist registration
	Meets national pharmacist registration requirements (GPhC in UK if accredited; ACPE/CCAPP in US/Canada)
	Common Regions
	India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Malaysia, parts of Middle East
	UK, Ireland, Australia, NZ, parts of EU
	EU (Hungary, Poland, Baltics), US, Canada, Japan, Saudi Arabia, Philippines
	Career Orientation
	Industry, manufacturing, regulatory affairs, community pharmacy
	Community/hospital pharmacy, industry, academia, regulatory
	Clinical pharmacy, hospital practice, ambulatory care, specialist prescribing
	Further Study Pathways
	MPharm, MSc, PharmD, PhD
	PhD, clinical specialisation, prescribing qualifications
	Post‑graduate residencies (PGY1/PGY2), fellowships, PhD
	Example Institutions
	University of Mumbai (India), University of Malaya (Malaysia)
	UCL, King’s, Nottingham, Trinity College Dublin
	University of Szeged (Hungary), Semmelweis University (Hungary), University of Toronto (Canada), University of California San Francisco (USA)
